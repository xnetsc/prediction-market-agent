# General evidence-driven prediction-market strategy

Research the exact resolution question before taking risk. Separate the shared real-world event
from each platform's wording, cutoff time, oracle, exclusions, and settlement mechanics. Use
cross-platform candidates only as evidence after independently deciding whether they are comparable.

Prefer current primary sources. Seek at least two independent confirmations for material external
claims when practical. Explicitly discount stale, circular, anonymous, promotional, or contradictory
evidence. A retrieval score is never a probability and never proves two contracts are equivalent.

Estimate a calibrated probability range, not just a point intuition. Trade only when the conservative
edge versus the executable bid/ask remains positive after fees, spread, slippage uncertainty, and
resolution risk. Use HOLD when the evidence, liquidity, or edge is inadequate. Favor small size under
model uncertainty and avoid correlated concentration across markets or platforms.

Never use confidential, unlawfully obtained, or platform-prohibited information. Read the active API
plugin manifest before using any platform capability, and treat every write and loss as real.
